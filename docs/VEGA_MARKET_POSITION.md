# Vega Market Position

Vega should be positioned as an AI Sales Operating System for SMBs, agencies, and managed-service teams.

She is not only a lead-generation tool. The defensible product idea is that Vega connects the operating chain:

1. Market discovery
2. Qualification
3. Opportunity Intelligence
4. Channel decisioning
5. Sender-governed outreach
6. Follow-up sequencing
7. Human phone coordination
8. Reply and booking handoff
9. Outcome history
10. Campaign learning

## Category

Vega sits between several established categories:

- GTM data workbenches such as Clay
- Autonomous outbound agents such as Artisan, 11x, and Gojiberry
- LinkedIn and warm-signal tools such as Traxy
- Inbound qualification and booking agents such as SalesAPE

The strongest market position is:

> Vega identifies the best opportunities, decides the safest next move, automates digital follow-up, and directs humans where conversations create the most value.

## Where Vega Should Win

Vega should not compete on cheap lead volume. The product should win on:

- explainable Opportunity Intelligence
- trust-based autonomy
- source and sender-quality controls
- safe first-touch and follow-up separation
- call-first and manual-contact lanes
- phone-assist accountability
- managed Ghost/VA fulfillment
- campaign isolation across clients
- Slack-native operating control
- outcome learning over successive cycles

## Where Vega Still Needs Proof

Vega should not claim category leadership until the system repeatedly proves:

- qualified conversations can be generated safely
- human phone tasks materially increase meeting rates
- campaign learning improves results across cycles
- booked meetings are verified calendar outcomes
- revenue can be attributed back to source, offer, channel, and message

## Recommended Wedge

Start below enterprise SDR automation:

- local and regional B2B service companies
- marketing, website, and automation agencies
- contractors pursuing commercial accounts
- professional-service firms
- businesses with an assistant, VA, or salesperson available for follow-up

Best-fit buyer:

- 5 to 75 employees
- average deal value above $2,500
- recurring pipeline need
- weak or inconsistent outbound
- willing to work phone-assist tasks or pay Ghost to work them

## Pricing Direction

Vega Scout faces price pressure if it is only positioned as lead discovery. Convert and Managed are the stronger differentiated offers.

- Vega Scout: qualified opportunity intelligence
- Vega Reach: managed safe outbound
- Vega Convert: outreach plus human-conversion workflow
- Vega Managed: Ghost-operated acquisition department

## Product Rule

Every future Vega feature should improve one of these production outcomes:

- safer qualified outreach
- better contact confidence
- more conversations
- better phone follow-up completion
- more meeting requests
- more booked and attended meetings
- stronger source, offer, and message learning
